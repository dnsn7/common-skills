# Venue and ranking verification

Verify the venue before writing the cover.

## Conferences

1. Confirm the paper appears in the official conference proceedings or program.
2. For CCF classification, use the current official CCF recommended conference/journal directory and record its edition or access date.
3. Write `会议简称 年份｜CCF 等级` only after both facts are verified.

## Journals

1. Confirm journal, volume, year, and DOI from the journal or publisher page.
2. Use stable descriptions such as `Nature Portfolio 医学期刊` when appropriate.
3. If showing JCR, CAS/中科院 partition, or impact factor, verify the exact edition/year and display it. Do not combine rankings from different years.

## Safe fallback

If no authoritative ranking is confirmed, show only the verified venue and year. Never guess a CCF class, quartile, impact factor, “顶会”, or “顶刊” label.
